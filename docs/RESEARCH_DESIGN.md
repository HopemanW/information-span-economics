# Research Design Roadmap

## Economic object

Information **span** is breadth: the fraction of economically relevant borrower dimensions that become standardized, observable, and transferable. Information **precision** is quality conditional on a dimension being covered.

A central prediction to test is that relationship-specific or specialized information rents fall as span expands, even if better precision can improve screening.

## Empirical ladder

1. Build and validate text-derived span/precision measures against hand labels.
2. Establish a quasi-experimental shock that changes the transferability of information.
3. Estimate average effects with conventional event-study/IV/DML methods.
4. Use causal forests only for heterogeneity conditional on the design.
5. Map reduced-form estimates into a structural lender-competition model only after the empirical moments are credible.

## Potential extensions

- open-banking/data-portability reforms;
- credit registries;
- adoption of standardized reporting or e-invoicing;
- lender IT/software rollouts;
- small-business financial-data APIs;
- cross-market differences in public-information infrastructure.
